▶ Video Checker Checks short videos before they're posted

Check a video before it's posted

A tool that checks finished short videos before they're posted, and lists anything that needs fixing and exactly where it happens. Below are videos it has checked.

  1. 1Upload a finished video
  2. 2It's checked against four rules
  3. 3You get a list of what to fix, and where

This is a read-only showcase of videos we checked. Checking a new video runs on our own machine; to run it yourself, see the code on GitHub.

What we check

  • 1

    Logo stays on screen

    Our Agency's logo is a watermark that should stay visible for the whole video, so we look for it every second.

  • 2

    Opening line starts quickly

    The hook is the spoken opening line that makes people keep watching. It has to start by 0:03, before viewers scroll away.

  • 3

    Footage matches what's being said

    B-roll is the extra footage shown while someone talks. When the speaker mentions something, the picture should show that, not something else.

  • 4

    No silent gaps

    Dead air is a stretch where nobody speaks. Gaps longer than 1 second make viewers lose interest.
